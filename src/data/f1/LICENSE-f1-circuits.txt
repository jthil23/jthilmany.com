Circuit outlines in circuits.json are derived from "f1-circuits" by Tomislav Bacinger
https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits (f1-circuits.geojson, MIT License).

Modifications: a subset of circuits was selected; longitude/latitude were projected to
local meters (equirectangular projection around each circuit's bounding-box center,
x east, y south) and the closed centerline was resampled to points spaced ~20 m apart
and rounded to whole meters.

The race calendar snapshot in calendar-2026.json is a trimmed copy of the Jolpica F1 API
response for https://api.jolpi.ca/ergast/f1/2026.json (retrieved 2026-10-04), used as a
build fallback when the live API is unavailable.
